A beginner-friendly web application that converts text, URLs, email addresses and phone numbers into QR codes.

Project Highlights

  • Generate QR codes in multiple sizes.
  • Customize foreground and background colors.
  • Download QR codes as PNG images.
  • View recent QR codes from local browser history.
  • Switch between dark and light themes.
